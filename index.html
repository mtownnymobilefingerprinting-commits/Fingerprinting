Frequently Asked Questions

Do you travel to our office or facility?

Yes. Mobile, on-site service is our specialty — we bring all equipment to your location anywhere in Middletown and Orange County, NY.

Do you offer both Live Scan and ink fingerprinting?

Yes. We can accommodate whichever format your licensing board, employer, or background check provider requires.

Can you fingerprint an entire department or new-hire class at once?

Yes. We regularly run bulk/group sessions for staffing agencies, healthcare employers, and schools. Contact us to schedule a block appointment.

How fast are results available?

Live Scan submissions are typically processed faster than mailed ink cards, though turnaround ultimately depends on the receiving agency.

What should employees bring to their appointment?

A valid government-issued photo ID and any required agency paperwork (ORI number, applicant forms, etc.), which we confirm with you ahead of time.

Do you work with staffing agencies and HR departments directly?

Yes — we can set up standing service agreements for recurring hiring cycles.
